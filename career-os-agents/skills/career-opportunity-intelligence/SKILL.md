# Career Opportunity Intelligence

Apply hard filters before scoring: closed/expired roles, impossible eligibility or location, and exact submitted/rejected duplicates.

Score with the approved high-aggressiveness weights: role fit 20, eligibility 15, strategic value 15, evidence fit 10, project bridge 10, timing 10, relationship leverage 10, resume readiness 5, source confidence 5.

- Apply now at 78+, prepare then apply at 62+.
- Produce a rich role brief: requirements, matched evidence, gaps, resume angle, warm paths, events, complexity, sequence, effort, and project bridge.
- Keep one canonical opportunity across duplicate signals.
- Preserve provenance and make feedback reversible.
- Queue application, resume, relationship, event, project-spec, and planning work when justified.

Do not create final submissions or send outreach.
